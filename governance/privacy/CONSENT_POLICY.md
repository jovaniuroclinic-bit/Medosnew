# Consent Policy

Consent must be explicit, granular, signed, time-bounded and revocable.

Consent documents are immutable. Changes are append-only events.

Consent states:

- PENDING
- AUTHORIZED
- REVOKED
- EXPIRED
- REJECTED
- SUPERSEDED

Recording-session states are independent:

- CREATED
- RECORDING
- PAUSED
- STOPPED
- ABORTED
