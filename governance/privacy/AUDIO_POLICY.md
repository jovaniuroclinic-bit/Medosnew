# Audio Policy

Audio capture is disabled by default.

MEDOS must not perform covert recording, indiscriminate surveillance, voice-biometric identification by default, lie detection, diagnosis, or automated guilt and intent determinations.

The Debian PRoot profile supports synthetic audio and file fixtures only. A future native Android bridge requires explicit consent, visible recording indication, revocation handling, platform permissions and independent testing.

> Este sistema no determina verdad, mentira, culpabilidad, intención ni diagnóstico. Los resultados acústicos son estimaciones probabilísticas, pueden ser incorrectos y requieren revisión humana.
